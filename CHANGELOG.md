# Changelog

All notable changes to `vtprojekty-cz` will be documented here.

This project follows semantic versioning once published.

## 1.0.1

- Updated `@trebired/frontend` to 29.5.0. A frame the site did not declare, such as one a script adds to the page after it loads, is removed before it shows and reported in the browser console. The lunch menu and the maps carry the declaration and load as before.

## 1.0.0

- Released the VT projekty website: Restaurace Kulturní dům and Kavárna Gól in Střílky, Koupaliště Koryčany, and accommodation in Střílky and Lískovec, prerendered in Czech at the unprefixed URLs and English under `/en`.
- Added the company landing page and three areas, Gastronomie, Volný čas and Ubytování, as link categories: header groups that open each area's sub-pages, footer columns and sections of the phone menu.
- Added a page for every venue, a celebrations page and a contact page, with breadcrumbs on every sub-page, the restaurant's lunch menu embedded from Meníčka, photograph galleries, maps and the pool's facilities, linking to koupaliste-korycany.cz for its prices and opening hours.
- Added the VT projekty logo, full and minimal, in a light and a dark tone, a favicon made from the minimal logo, and a social card.
- Added a privacy page, linked from the footer, the operator's company details in the footer and on the contact page, and the consumer dispute body on the contact page.
- Added "Vytvořil Trebired" at the end of the footer ("Made by Trebired" in English), linking to trebired.com in a new tab.
- Added a click before the Meníčka menu and the Google maps load, and contact buttons that go to the contact page instead of opening a call or an email.
- Added a home page hero that cuts the VT mark into photographs of the restaurant and the pool, one photograph in each of its shapes.
- Added per-page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, language alternates, Open Graph locales with their countries, structured data for the organization, the website, each venue and each sub-page's breadcrumb trail in the page's own language, and a sitemap, checked by the SEO audit on every build.
